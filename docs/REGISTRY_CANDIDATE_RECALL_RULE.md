## 1. Full source universe

The source is `product_codes_HS92_V202401b.csv` in CEPII BACI V202401b. The
source member has 5,022 rows: 5,021 numeric six-digit product codes and the
legacy non-WCO row `9999AA`. Every row must be scanned. The source hash, row

Here, **full-table scan** means that the frozen regular expressions are applied

The review unit is `(chain_id, hs6)`, because one HS6 description can be a
candidate for more than one chain.

## 3. Observable-attribution decision

The official BACI HS92 description is the sole adjudicative evidence. External
knowledge may clarify a term but may not supply an absent commodity, material,
species, processing form, or end use.

- **Include** when the description explicitly identifies the focal commodity,
  material, or species and the product form lies within one frozen stage.
- **Commodity-explicit blends remain eligible**, regardless of stated focal
  share. The recorded value is the complete published HS6 basket; no
  within-code focal share is estimated.
- **Commodity-explicit residual or n.e.s. baskets remain eligible** when their
  form lies inside a stage. Inclusion does not imply basket homogeneity.
- **Exclude** generic, negative, multi-commodity, mixed-species, or
  unresolved-material descriptions that do not isolate the focal commodity or
  material.
- **Out of stage** is recorded separately when the focal commodity is explicit
  but the product form is outside every declared stage.

Classification is ordered. Observable attribution is tested before stage fit.
A focal word that occurs only as optional content, a negated material, one item
in a multi-commodity or multi-use list, or the end use of a different traded
product is **exclude**, not out of stage. **Out of stage** is available only
after the traded product's focal attribution has been resolved.

No unresolved record is permitted when the revision is accepted.

## 4. Frozen boundary resolutions

The following boundaries remove the remaining degrees of freedom:

- Sheep wool yarn includes yarn explicitly identifying wool regardless of wool
  share. `wool or fine animal hair` remains unresolved and is excluded.
- Raw sheep or lamb skins and furskins are inside `exp_rawskin` when the
  description itself says raw; tanned or dressed skins are outside that stage.
- The frozen wool-form ontology covers wool not carded or combed, non-crude
  wool grease and its derivatives, wool tops and other combed wool, and wool
  yarn. Crude wool grease and carded-wool fibre are observable wool products
  but remain out of stage. This is an analytical-family boundary, not a claim
  that those forms are unrelated to wool.
- Cotton yarn and woven fabric include every description explicitly identifying
  cotton, regardless of HS chapter or cotton share, but the official description
  must itself identify the required yarn or woven-fabric form. `chenille fabric`
  and `gauze` do not silently supply `woven`. A yarn whose primary named fibre
  is polyester, acrylic, synthetic, or artificial remains a cotton-explicit
  yarn basket when the description also says it is mixed mainly or solely with
  cotton; it is not relabelled as pure or cotton-primary yarn. Cotton knitted
  fabric is handled analogously.
- Cotton knitted-apparel scope is limited to men's/boys' shirts; T-shirts,
  singlets and other vests; men's/boys' and women's/girls' trousers and related
  lower-body garments; and jerseys, pullovers, cardigans, waistcoats and
  similar articles. The reference HS6 set is `610510, 610910, 610342, 610462,
  611020`.
- Cotton non-knitted-apparel scope is limited to men's/boys' shirts and
  men's/boys' or women's/girls' trousers and related lower-body garments. The
  reference HS6 set is `620520, 620342, 620462`.
- Cotton homewares cover cotton bed linen and cotton kitchen or toilet linen.
  Table linen, curtains, tents, bags, blankets, and other cotton articles are
  out of stage.
- Aluminium semis wire means basic non-stranded wire. Stranded wire, cable,
  plaited band, and finished articles are out of stage.
- Aluminium attribution follows the same basket rule: a code listing
  fluoroaluminates alongside fluorosilicates and other complex salts is
  excluded, while a refractory ceramic basket whose every branch explicitly
  contains alumina is attributable but outside the declared stages.
- Cross-chapter commodity-explicit blends remain eligible. In particular, a
  copper-nickel product is not excluded because it appears in the copper
  chapter when nickel is explicit and its form falls within a nickel stage.
  These are described as nickel-explicit copper-nickel alloy baskets, never as
  nickel-only or necessarily nickel-base products.
- Machinery that merely lists cocoa among several manufactured objects is
  excluded: cocoa is an optional end use of the traded machine, not an
  attributed material/product basket.

This fixes the narrow, form-defined cotton-apparel interpretation. It does not
use the broader all-Chapter-61/62 interpretation.

The apparel and homeware restrictions deliberately freeze the pre-audit form
ontology. They retain representative basic garments and bed or kitchen/toilet
linen whose product form and cotton attribution are explicit at HS6. This is a
precision/stage-definition choice, not a claim to recall every cotton garment
or household article, and it is not justified by trade values or model results.
