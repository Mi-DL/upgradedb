# Registry human-review completion addendum

This document reports the post-review status of the outcome-blind registry
validation. The operational codebook in
[`REGISTRY_REVIEW_CODEBOOK.md`](REGISTRY_REVIEW_CODEBOOK.md) is preserved
byte-for-byte as an immutable pre-review input: its pending-status paragraph
records the state at the review freeze and is not the current completion
record. Current status is governed by
[`registry_curation_protocol.json`](../chains/evidence/registry_curation_protocol.json)
and the hash-bound
[`registry_human_review_receipt.json`](../chains/evidence/registry_human_review_receipt.json).

## Completed scope

- Two reviewers independently applied the frozen ontology to the
  outcome-blind stratified probability sample of 212 decision records drawn
  from the 610-record machine-assigned frame.
- The review also covered a separate census of all 53 stage definitions.
- The reviewers' original `Yes`/`No`/`Uncertain` verdicts agreed on 192 of
  212 sampled decisions (90.6%; Cohen's kappa = 0.595).
- Discussion-based adjudication resolved every `No` or `Uncertain` verdict,
  retained every frozen proposal, and accepted no registry construct change.
  Existing cohorts and experimental results therefore remain applicable.

Kappa summarizes agreement on the three original verdict categories
(`Yes`, `No`, and `Uncertain`). It is not an accuracy estimate for proposal
statuses, registry inclusion labels, or the complete machine-assigned ledger.

## Coverage boundary

The remaining 398 machine-assigned decisions were not individually reviewed
by a human. Their error rate, and consequently the full-ledger error rate,
remain unknown. The completed review also does not constitute an independent
search over all possible lexicons or a manual census of all 5,022 BACI source
descriptions. Agreement and completion claims are limited to the frozen
212-record sample and the 53-definition census.
